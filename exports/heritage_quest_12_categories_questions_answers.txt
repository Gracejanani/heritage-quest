Difficulty: Medium

Q3. Why are miniature paintings useful cultural sources?
Answer: They reveal patronage, costume, court life, literary themes and artistic conventions
Difficulty: Medium

Q4. What is one reason handloom and craft traditions vary strongly by region?
Answer: Local materials, climate, patronage, community skills and trade histories shape production
Difficulty: Medium

Q5. Why is oral transmission important in many performing arts?
Answer: Knowledge can be taught through demonstration, memory and teacher-student practice beyond written notation
Difficulty: Medium

Q6. What does the diversity of Indian painting traditions demonstrate?
Answer: Art developed through multiple regional courts, communities, religious settings and markets
Difficulty: Medium

Q7. Why should historians be cautious when labeling a tradition 'unchanged' or 'pure'?
Answer: Cultural forms often adapt through migration, patronage, technology and exchange
Difficulty: Advanced

Q8. How can patronage shape artistic style?
Answer: Patrons influence themes, resources, workshop organization and audiences while artists also exercise creativity
Difficulty: Advanced

Q9. Why is intangible cultural heritage harder to preserve than a stone monument?
Answer: It depends on active practitioners, transmission and social contexts rather than only physical conservation
